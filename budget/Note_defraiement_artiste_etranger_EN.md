## 1. Why this agreement

Monique Festival is a young festival with strong **ethical and environmental commitments**. Welcoming international artists is part of our DNA, but we want to do so in a way that is consistent with our **environmental charter** and our limited budget as a first-edition non-profit.

This agreement defines how we reimburse your travel expenses, in alignment with our principles:

- **Encouraging low-carbon travel** as the default
- **Capping financial impact** to keep the festival sustainable
- **Providing comfort on-site** (food + accommodation in kind), so the only thing we ask you to organize is your transport

## 3. Our environmental commitment on transport

Monique Festival's **Ethical Charter** prioritizes low-impact travel. As a result, our reimbursement policy reflects this:

### ✅ Eligible transport modes *(reimbursed within the cap)*

- **Train** — preferred mode, including international (Eurostar, Thalys, ICE, TGV, regional trains)
- **Long-distance coach / bus** — FlixBus, BlaBlaCar Bus, etc.
- **Plane** — only when no reasonable rail alternative exists *(typically: trips with no direct rail connection or where total rail journey exceeds ~12 hours)*

### ❌ Non-eligible transport modes *(not reimbursed)*

- **Personal car** *(driving alone or with one passenger)* — high carbon footprint per person
- **Taxi or rideshare** for the long-distance leg *(local taxi to/from station is fine if needed)*

### 🍽️ What is provided in kind on-site *(no separate reimbursement)*

- **Accommodation** at La Grange Huguenet (festival venue, Besançon)
- **All meals** during the festival period (28-30 August 2026)

This in-kind provision is part of our welcoming approach: you only need to focus on getting here.

---

## 4. Reimbursement cap and mechanism

- **Maximum reimbursement**: **€200 per person**, regardless of actual cost
- **Mechanism**: refund of **actual costs**, on presentation of supporting documents, **up to** the cap

| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| Actual transport cost = €80 | We reimburse **€80** *(only actual cost)* |
| Actual transport cost = €180 | We reimburse **€180** *(actual cost, under cap)* |
| Actual transport cost = €250 | We reimburse **€200** *(capped — Performer covers €50 difference)* |

### Why this approach

- It rewards careful planning *(booking early gets you better prices and reduces our financial impact)*
- It keeps our budget predictable
- It is fully transparent — we never reimburse more than what you actually paid

## 6. Tax and accounting

This reimbursement is a **professional expense reimbursement**, not income, in accordance with French tax doctrine (BOI-IR-RICI-250). It is not subject to social charges or income tax for the Performer.

The Association keeps the supporting documents in its accounting records for the legal duration (10 years).
